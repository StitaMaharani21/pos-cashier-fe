# modules/owner/stock-history

Riwayat Stok: every item whose stock is tracked, and the stock turnover of each — what came in and what went out.

**"Menipis" / "Habis"**: a bahan baku reads *Menipis* at or under its `min_stock`; a per-menu menu reads *Habis* at 0 and *Menipis* at or under its own Stok Minimum (`low_stock_threshold`, set in the menu form; default 5).

**List** (`section/StockHistorySection.tsx`): two tabs (`?tab=menu`, default Bahan Baku), each a server-paged, searchable table of items — Nama, Stok Saat Ini, and an "Aksi" column with *Lihat riwayat* and *Ubah stok*. **Access:** the page opens on every plan (`routeAccess` = menu module); the Menu tab works for everyone with the `menu` permission, the Bahan Baku tab only exists with Inventori (`inventory_full` + `inventory` permission). Bahan Baku uses `listIngredients`; Menu lists only menus counted per item (`listMenus({ stockDeductionMethod: "by_menu" })` → `GET /master/menus?stock_deduction_method=by_menu`). Menus that deduct through their recipe (`by_ingredient`) have no stock of their own — their turnover is the bahan baku's. Rows are normalised to `StockItem` (`lib/stock-item.ts`).

**Turnover** (`components/StockMovementsSheet.tsx`): a side Sheet per item with Total masuk / Total keluar cards (`total_in` / `total_out` of the movements endpoint — all pages, not affected by the filter), a Semua / Masuk / Keluar filter (`direction=in|out`) and the paginated movement table (`columns/stock-movement.columns.tsx`). Backed by `GET /master/ingredients/{id}/stock-movements` and `GET /master/menus/{id}/stock-movements` (owner-only).

**Direction and kind.** The backend stores `qty` as an absolute value; the signed change is `delta` (`stock_after − stock_before`, `lib/normalize.ts` falls back to computing it). The "Jenis" badge comes from `movementKind` (`lib/labels.ts`): `IN`+`PURCHASE` = *Stok masuk* (green), `IN`+`ORDER` = *Pembatalan pesanan* (stock restored when an order is cancelled, green), `OUT` = *Penjualan* (red), `ADJUSTMENT` = *Koreksi stok (+/−)* by the sign of `delta`. Bahan baku movements also show *Harga per Satuan* (`unit_cost`: the purchase price for goods received, the average cost for everything else; empty before cost tracking).

**Ubah Stok is the ONE place stock changes** (`components/StockChangeDialog.tsx`, opened by the row action and by the movements sheet's button), for bahan baku and menus counted per item alike. The menu form, the bahan baku form and the Bahan Baku page only show stock read-only and point here; there is no "Stok Awal" field anywhere — a new item starts at 0 and its first stock is a *Stok Masuk*. Two modes in one dialog: **Stok Masuk** (`components/StockInForm.tsx`: jumlah, harga beli per satuan for bahan baku — optional, keterangan; `POST /master/ingredients/{id}/stock-in` / `POST /master/menus/{id}/stock-in`, recorded as *Stok masuk*; the price updates the ingredient's harga beli, recalculates its weighted-average cost (HPP) and is written to the price history, all in one backend transaction; the form previews the new average via `lib/average-cost.ts`, the same formula as the backend) and **Koreksi** (`components/StockAdjustForm.tsx`: signed non-zero amount, required reason ≥3 characters; `PATCH /master/ingredients/{id}/stock` / `PATCH /master/menus/{id}/stock`, recorded as *Koreksi stok*). Both previews say when a menu will be marked unavailable / available again; adding stock to a menu that ran out makes it available again. Schemas: `schemas/stock-change.schema.ts`. After saving, `lib/invalidate-stock.ts` refreshes `ingredients`, `menus`, `stock-movements` and `stock-reconciliation`.

Every stock change leaves a movement: sales, cancellations, stock-in and corrections. Items changed before this was enforced were not recorded, so *Cek Selisih Stok* can still list them. (The backend still accepts `stock` on create and `PUT /master/menus/{id}/stock`, but the app no longer uses them.)

Sublayers: `api/` (movement lists, stock-in, corrections), `lib/` (`stock-item`, `normalize`, `labels`, `average-cost`, `invalidate-stock`), `components/` (`StockMovementsSheet`, `StockChangeDialog`, `StockInForm`, `StockAdjustForm`), `columns/` (`stock-item.columns`, `stock-movement.columns`), `schemas/` (`stock-change.schema.ts`), `section/` (`StockHistorySection.tsx`).
